REDNOTE · XIAOHONGSHU GUIDE

Save RedNote videos
and photo notes on iPhone.

Copy a RedNote (Xiaohongshu) share link and paste it into VidKeep. No sign-in is needed to try saving public video notes and photo notes.

For the VidKeep iPhone and iPad app by Jintao Lv · Updated November 5, 2026

Save it in four steps

  1. Copy the note link

    Open the public note in RedNote, tap Share in the top corner, then Copy link. Short xhslink links and the full share text both work.

  2. Paste it into VidKeep

    Open VidKeep and paste the link, or choose VidKeep from the share sheet. You do not need a RedNote account.

  3. Keep the app open

    Photo notes need VidKeep open until they finish saving. For video notes, the file transfer can continue in the background once the video is found.

  4. Check offline playback

    Once the download completes, test it without a connection. If you only see a title and cover, you have a bookmark, not a local file.

Not sure a link will work?

Paste it into the link checker on the home page. The check runs in your browser only; the link is not uploaded and nothing is downloaded by the website.

What does a saved photo note look like?

Photo notes keep their original picture order, with zoom and reading progress. Live Photos are saved as still images.

Live streams and whole collections are not supported; save individual notes instead.

Questions

Why did a note fail to save?

The post may have been removed, or be private, sign-in-only, paid, restricted or verification-required. Live streams are not supported. VidKeep does not bypass these restrictions, and Pro does not change them. Some temporary failures can be retried.

Can I still watch it if the original is deleted?

Yes, if the download completed and the local file is still on your device. VidKeep cannot recover a post that was removed before you saved it.

Is it free?

Saving links is unlimited and you get 5 free video downloads a day. Organizing, playback, timestamped notes and ordinary backups are free. Monthly, yearly or one-time Lifetime Pro removes the daily download limit; source restrictions still apply.
