隐私政策

运营者：Jintao Lv（App Store 开发者 jintao LV）
更新日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日

保存在你的设备上

视频、原链接、分享文案、作者与标题、分类、合集目录、选集与下载任务进度、播放进度、备注和时间点笔记保存在本机。当前无需注册开发者账户，没有开发者云同步，不接入广告、跨 App 追踪或第三方统计 SDK。

链接与网络访问

只有你主动粘贴时才读取剪贴板。系统分享入口只接收你选择分享的内容。新收藏可自动向来源平台及其图片服务请求公开标题、作者和封面；预览本身不会下载视频。你可以在设置中关闭自动预览，也可以在收藏详情中手动刷新。请求会向相应服务暴露该链接、IP 地址和必要的网络信息，适用其隐私政策。下载时，设备会读取来源的公开作品页，或直接向来源平台的播放服务请求视频流，再从来源下载到本机，不经过开发者或第三方中转服务器。读取网页时使用临时网页空间，不使用你的平台登录账户，不处理验证码。读取支持的抖音公开合集目录时也直接请求来源平台，只有你选集确认后才开始下载所选视频；目录与任务状态保存在本机。运营者不接收预览、解析和视频请求，也不保存你的视频。你主动打开原链接时，由系统打开对应平台，其自身的隐私政策适用于该次访问。你主动打开隐私、条款和支持网页时，网站服务商可能处理必要的网络请求信息。

私密收藏与独立密码

私密收藏使用你单独设置的 6 位数字密码，不使用手机锁屏密码代替。密码校验资料保存在仅本设备的系统钥匙串中，不保存明文密码，也不发布给分享扩展或写入普通备份。你可主动开启 Face ID / Touch ID 快捷解锁；识别由系统处理，App 不读取生物特征。分享扩展只获知主 App 是否已设置私密密码，可以按你的选择私密保存，但不能浏览私密内容。

关闭私密区或 App 进入后台后上锁；私密标题、封面和笔记不进入普通浏览与搜索。忘记密码时，必须明确确认删除本机私密内容后才能重新设置。此功能是 App 内访问隔离，不是独立文件加密。以前导出的副本、相册内容、来源平台内容和系统设备备份不会因本机设为私密或重置密码而自动改变。

购买

订阅、一次性买断和恢复购买由 Apple 处理。应用在设备上验证交易以判断权益。运营者不会获取你的 Apple 账户密码或银行卡信息，当前没有用于保存购买记录的开发者服务器。

导入、备份与删除

从文件选择器导入时，只处理你选中的文件。导出视频或普通收藏备份后，数据由你选择的应用或文件服务处理。手动和自动普通备份包含普通视频、分类、合集、进度和笔记，排除私密内容与合集下载状态，公开单集链接可保留为普通书签；可以保留普通合集来源信息，以便恢复后重新获取目录和选集。普通备份未加密，请保管好文件。系统设备备份是否包含数据取决于系统设置，不能把普通备份排除私密内容理解为所有系统备份也排除私密内容。

你可以在 App 内删除收藏和视频。删除合集不会删除视频。卸载应用会删除应用内的本地数据，我们无法恢复没有备份的内容。没有需要向开发者申请注销的 VidKeep 账户。

联系与网站

支持页面提供 GitHub 公开问题入口，需要 GitHub 账户。请勿提交私人视频、敏感链接、联系方式、支付信息或账户凭据。你主动提交的内容由 GitHub 按其政策托管；网站由 GitHub Pages 提供，GitHub 可能处理必要的访问日志。本网站不放置广告。

官网访问统计

官网使用运营者自己的统计服务（位于新加坡），按 UTC 日期汇总页面浏览、可识别的来源类别（如 ChatGPT、搜索引擎或来源未知）和前往 App Store 的点击次数。发送内容仅为本站预定义页面、事件类型、来源类别和测试标记，不发送完整来源网址、查询参数、搜索词、页面标题、视频链接、用户标识或账户信息。点击不代表下载或购买，来源信息也可能缺失。

网站不设置统计 Cookie，不生成访客标识，不进行跨网站追踪。浏览器的会话存储仅保存一个来源类别，用于在同一标签页内浏览本站时保留来源；关闭该标签页会清除它。启用 Do Not Track 或 Global Privacy Control 的浏览器不会发送上述统计。网络服务需要处理 IP 地址；统计表不保存 IP，防滥用限流使用 IP 的密钥摘要，按 2 天的保留窗口定期清理。统计请求不携带 Cookie，也不发送 Referer 标头。聚合计数按 30 天的保留窗口定期清理，备份按既有周期到期清理；官网统计与 App 内设备统计分开，不关联反馈或购买记录。

iOS App 内反馈与使用统计

提供反馈功能的 iOS 版本会在你点击发送时，将反馈正文、选填的联系方式和你保留在正文中的链接发送到运营者位于新加坡的服务器。报告下载问题会预填来源链接、错误类别和 App/iOS 版本；你可以编辑或删除这些内容后再发送。反馈不会公开发布，不需要注册账号，仅供你和获授权的客服查看。

“使用与下载统计”默认开启，可随时在设置中关闭。统计包含 App 打开、下载问题报告次数，以及按来源、成功或失败结果、错误类别汇总的每日计数与 App 版本，不发送视频链接、标题、作者、视频文件或资料库内容。仅使用来源和错误的预定义类别；原始错误文本不会作为统计发送。数据用于发现来源失效、改进稳定性和了解功能使用，不用于广告、个性化营销、出售数据或跨 App 追踪，也不接入第三方统计 SDK。

反馈与统计使用本机随机生成的同一个 App 专用设备标识及认证凭证，保存在本设备钥匙串中；不是广告标识，也不是 Apple 账户或硬件序列号。该标识可将同一设备的反馈与统计关联，如果你提供联系方式，也可以与这些信息关联。因此这些数据不属于完全不可关联的匿名数据。服务提供商处理网络连接所必需的信息，包括 IP 地址；本服务不使用 IP 推断地理位置。

关闭统计会停止后续统计上传并清空本机待发送计数，不影响发送反馈，也不会自动删除此前已发送的数据。在“我的 → 意见反馈”选择“删除我的反馈记录”，可删除本设备的对话、消息和统计，并清除本机反馈凭证。若之后继续开启统计或发送反馈，会生成新的标识。服务端统计保留 30 天，反馈保留至用户删除；必要的禁用身份标记、安全审计和备份按服务保留周期处理。审计保留 180 天，包含操作类型及必要标识，不包含反馈正文；备份到期清理，删除不会即时抹除既有备份。卸载 App 不会自动发起服务器删除；如需删除，请先在 App 内操作。以上服务不改变本机视频和私密内容的存储方式。

未成年人和政策变化

VidKeep 是通用视频管理工具，不面向儿童提供内容推荐，也不要求填写年龄或身份信息。若后续加入新的数据处理功能，将更新本政策及商店隐私信息，并在需要时征求同意。

Privacy policy — English

Operator: Jintao Lv. Updated October 30, 2026. Videos, links, shared text, metadata, categories, collection directories, selections, download progress and notes are stored on your device. The app requires no developer account and uses no automatic developer cloud sync, advertisements, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDK.

Clipboard contents are read only when you choose to paste. New bookmarks can request public titles, authors and cover images from the source platform and its image services. Previews themselves do not download video files. Turn off automatic previews in Settings or refresh a preview from item details. Requests reveal the link, IP address and necessary network information to those services under their privacy policies. For downloads, your device loads the public work page or asks the source platform’s playback service for video streams, then transfers the video directly from that source without a developer or third-party relay server. Web pages use temporary web storage. Resolution does not use your platform account or solve verification challenges. Supported public Douyin collection directories are also requested directly from the source. Episode downloads start only after your selection; directory and task state stay on your device. These requests are not sent to the operator. Opening an original link hands it to the system and source platform, whose policy applies. Visiting privacy, terms or support pages contacts their web hosts. Apple processes subscriptions, one-time purchases and purchase restoration; VidKeep verifies transactions on device and does not receive passwords or payment-card details.

Private Collection uses your separate 6-digit PIN, not the device passcode. PIN verification data is kept in the system Keychain on this device, never as plaintext or in ordinary backups or the share extension. Face ID/Touch ID is optional and handled by the system; the app does not read biometric data. The extension knows only whether a private PIN has been configured and cannot browse private items. The private area locks when closed or when the app enters the background; its content stays out of ordinary browsing and search. Forgetting the PIN requires confirming deletion of local private content before creating a new PIN. This is in-app access isolation, not separate file encryption. Existing exports, Photos copies, source-platform content and system backups are not changed by hiding items or resetting the PIN.

Imports are selected by you. Exports and ordinary backups go to your chosen destination. Manual and automatic ordinary backups are unencrypted. They exclude private content and collection download state, retain public episode links as ordinary bookmarks, and can keep ordinary collection source information for a fresh directory lookup and selection after restore. System backups depend on device settings and may contain data excluded from ordinary backups. Delete content within the app; uninstalling removes local app data. We cannot restore unbacked-up content.

Website analytics

This website uses the operator’s own analytics service in Singapore to count page views, recognized source categories such as ChatGPT, search engines or unknown sources, and clicks that open the App Store. Counts are grouped by UTC date. Requests contain only a predefined website page, event type, source category and test flag. They do not contain full referrer URLs, query strings, search terms, page titles, video links, user identifiers or account details. A click is not a download or purchase, and source information may be missing.

We do not set analytics cookies, create visitor identifiers or track visitors across websites. Session storage keeps only a source category while you browse this website in the same tab; closing the tab clears it. Browsers with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control enabled do not send these analytics. Network providers process IP addresses to deliver requests. Analytics tables do not store IP addresses; abuse prevention uses keyed IP digests, periodically cleared on a 2-day retention window. Analytics requests omit cookies and the Referer header. Aggregate counts are periodically cleared on a 30-day retention window; backups expire on their existing schedule. Website counts are separate from in-app device statistics and are not linked to feedback or purchases.

In-app feedback and usage statistics on iOS

iOS versions with this feature send feedback to the operator’s server in Singapore only when you choose Send. This includes the message, optional contact details and links you leave in it. Reporting a download problem pre-fills the source link, error category and app/iOS version; you may edit or remove them before sending. Feedback is private to you and authorized support staff, and requires no account.

Usage and download statistics are enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings. They include app opens, download-problem reports and daily counts by source, success/failure and error category, plus app version. Statistics do not include video links, titles, authors, video files or library contents. Categories are predefined; raw error text is not sent as statistics. We use this information for support, reliability and usage analysis, not advertising, personalized marketing, data sales or cross-app tracking. We use no third-party analytics SDK.

Feedback and statistics share a random app-specific device identifier and credential held in this device’s Keychain. It is not an advertising ID, Apple Account or hardware serial number. It can associate statistics with feedback and contact details you choose to provide, so this data is not completely anonymous. Service providers process information needed for network connections, including IP addresses; this service does not use IP addresses to infer location.

Turning statistics off stops new statistical uploads and clears pending counts, without disabling feedback or deleting data already sent. In Me → Feedback, Delete my feedback removes this device’s conversations, messages and counts and clears its local feedback credential. Later use with statistics enabled or a new feedback message may create a new identifier. Statistics are retained for 30 days; feedback remains until deletion. Limited disabled-identity records, security audits and backups follow their retention schedules. Audits are retained for 180 days and contain action types and necessary identifiers, not feedback text. Backups expire on their retention schedule and are not immediately rewritten after deletion. Uninstalling does not request deletion from the server; use the in-app deletion option first. These services do not upload your local videos or private collection.

Public support is also available through GitHub issues. Do not include private media, sensitive URLs, contact details or credentials. GitHub hosts this site and support submissions and may retain operational logs. There are no advertisements here. VidKeep is a general-purpose tool. Material privacy changes will be reflected here and in store disclosures.
