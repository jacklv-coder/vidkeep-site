X · TWITTER VIDEO GUIDE

How to save X (Twitter)
videos on iPhone.

Copy the post link, paste it into VidKeep and wait for the download to finish. No X account is needed, and both x.com and twitter.com links work.

For the VidKeep iPhone and iPad app by Jintao Lv · Updated November 5, 2026

Save it in four steps

  1. Copy the post link

    Open the public post with the video in X, tap Share, then Copy link. It usually looks like x.com/username/status/1234…

  2. Paste it into VidKeep

    Open VidKeep and paste the link, or choose VidKeep from the share sheet in X.

  3. Keep the app open

    Keep VidKeep open while it finds the video and until the download finishes.

  4. Check offline playback

    A title and cover in your library are not the file itself. Once the download completes, test playback without a connection.

Not sure a link will work?

Paste it into the link checker on the home page. The check runs in your browser only; the link is not uploaded and nothing is downloaded by the website.

What if a post has several videos?

Copy the link to the specific video you want, then paste that into VidKeep. Live streams and restricted posts are not supported.

Questions

Why did an X video fail to download?

The post may have been removed, or be private, sign-in-only, paid, restricted or verification-required. Live streams are not supported. VidKeep does not bypass these restrictions, and Pro does not change them. Some temporary failures can be retried.

Can I still watch it if the original is deleted?

Yes, if the download completed and the local file is still on your device. VidKeep cannot recover a post that was removed before you saved it.

Is it free?

Saving links is unlimited and you get 5 free video downloads a day. Organizing, playback, timestamped notes and ordinary backups are free. Monthly, yearly or one-time Lifetime Pro removes the daily download limit; source restrictions still apply.

Save unlimited links and download 5 videos a day for free. Only save content you own or have permission to keep. VidKeep is independent and not affiliated with X.
