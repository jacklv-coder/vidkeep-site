A LOCAL FILE, NOT JUST A LINK

Keep it before the original disappears.

Saving a bookmark does not save the video. Once a download is complete, you can keep watching if the original is removed, as long as the local file remains. VidKeep cannot recover an original that was removed before you downloaded it.

YOUR OWN LIBRARY

Your picks. At your pace.

A calmer screen for the videos you choose

Explore, Study and Kids modes play selected saved videos without an endless recommendation feed. Set a daily viewing limit and an optional parent check. VidKeep is a general-purpose tool; it does not supply or choose children's content.

Private items, a separate PIN

Private Collection keeps private videos and notes out of ordinary browsing and search. Use a separate 6-digit PIN, with optional Face ID or Touch ID. It locks when closed or when the app enters the background.

This is in-app access protection, not separate file encryption. Ordinary backups exclude private content.

Source support, explained clearly.

Only save content you own or have permission to keep. Downloads depend on the original work, source availability, region and format. Check Supported Sources in the app before purchasing, and keep VidKeep open while it finds and processes a video.

Private, sign-in-only, paid, restricted, verification-required and live content is unsupported. Pro does not change those restrictions. VidKeep is not affiliated with the source platforms.

Supported public photo posts can retain their picture order, with zoom, reading progress and original-picture export. Eligible public Douyin collections offer episode selection, reuse of completed downloads and retries; this does not extend to every platform's playlists.
