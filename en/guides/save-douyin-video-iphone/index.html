DOUYIN VIDEO GUIDE

How to save Douyin
videos on iPhone.

Douyin is the Chinese version of TikTok. Copy its share link, paste it into VidKeep and wait for the download to finish. Public photo posts and collection episodes can be saved too.

For the VidKeep iPhone and iPad app by Jintao Lv · Updated November 5, 2026

Save it in four steps

  1. Copy the share link

    Open the public video in Douyin, tap Share, then Copy link. You can paste the whole share text; VidKeep finds the link inside it.

  2. Paste it into VidKeep

    Open VidKeep and paste, or choose VidKeep from the Douyin share sheet. New items start in Uncategorized.

  3. Keep the app open

    Keep VidKeep open while it looks up the video. Once found, the video file transfer can continue in the background.

  4. Check offline playback

    Once the download completes, test playback without a connection.

Not sure a link will work?

Paste it into the link checker on the home page. The check runs in your browser only; the link is not uploaded and nothing is downloaded by the website.

Can I save a whole Douyin collection?

For public collections with a complete directory, VidKeep lists the episodes first. Choose the ones you want; they are saved with their original episode numbers. Completed episodes are reused and missing ones can be filled in. Keep the app open while the directory loads; each episode counts separately toward the daily allowance.

Public photo posts keep their picture order. This collection support is specific to Douyin and does not extend to every platform's playlists.

Questions

Why did a Douyin link fail?

The post may have been removed, or be private, sign-in-only, paid, restricted or verification-required. Live streams are not supported. VidKeep does not bypass these restrictions, and Pro does not change them. Some temporary failures can be retried.

Can I still watch it if the original is deleted?

Yes, if the download completed and the local file is still on your device. VidKeep cannot recover a post that was removed before you saved it.

Is it free?

Saving links is unlimited and you get 5 free video downloads a day. Organizing, playback, timestamped notes and ordinary backups are free. Monthly, yearly or one-time Lifetime Pro removes the daily download limit; source restrictions still apply.
