VidKeep offers an optional Lifetime Pro purchase for iPhone and iPad. It removes the daily video download limit without recurring renewal. You can also use the free allowance or choose a monthly or yearly subscription.

For the VidKeep iPhone and iPad app by Jintao Lv · Updated October 30, 2026

Start with the free features

Save unlimited links, import your own local videos and download 5 videos daily. Categories, collections, playback, timestamped notes and ordinary backups are free. You can try whether your eligible source videos work before deciding to purchase.

Every video in a supported collection counts separately toward the daily allowance. Saving a link alone is not the same as downloading the file, and source restrictions apply to every plan.

Apple processes purchases. Available plans, local prices and confirmation details appear on the purchase screen. VidKeep Pro does not include developer cloud storage or change source restrictions.

Can I use VidKeep without a subscription?

Yes. Stay on the free allowance, or choose the optional one-time Lifetime purchase if you want to remove the daily download limit without renewing a subscription. Monthly and yearly subscriptions remain available as alternatives.

If a subscription expires, your existing content remains accessible. Expiry does not delete downloaded videos or notes. New downloads return to the free daily allowance.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel a subscription?

Open VidKeep Pro from Me and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel an auto-renewable subscription in your App Store subscription settings.

Lifetime is a non-consumable purchase and does not renew. Restoring a purchase restores the applicable entitlement; it does not restore your local video library.

A purchase is not a backup.

Your videos and notes are stored on your device. Export an ordinary collection backup to Files, or enable automatic backup to a folder you choose, such as iCloud Drive. Verify the backup before changing devices, deleting files or uninstalling.

Ordinary backups are unencrypted. They include ordinary videos, organization, progress and notes; they exclude private content and collection download state. Public episode links may remain bookmarks, and collections can retain source information for a fresh lookup and episode selection. System device backups follow system settings.

What does Pro not unlock?

A purchase cannot make a removed or unsupported video available. Private, sign-in-only, paid, restricted, verification-required and live works remain unsupported. Only download or import content you own or have permission to keep. Check Supported Sources in the app before purchasing.
